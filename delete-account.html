Delete your account

Tatties · Last updated: October 29, 2026

In short

An account in Tatties is optional. Deleting it removes your account and everything stored in the cloud. What is on your phone stays there until you remove it yourself.

In the app

  • Open Tatties and go to Settings > Account & cloud sync.
  • Tap Delete account and confirm.
  • Your account and all your cloud data are deleted straight away.

Without the app

Send an email to hello@tatties.app from the email address you use for your account, with the subject Delete my account. We delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account: your email address and sign-in details
  • All tattoos you synced, including photos, notes, costs and sessions
  • Your body: shape, age, height, weight and measurements
  • Your Founder number and any rewards

What stays

  • Data on your own device. Remove it with Settings > Erase all local data, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Anonymous daily counts, only if you said yes to them. They are not linked to your account and are removed after at most 13 months.
  • Feedback you sent from the app. It is not stored with your account; we keep it for at most 2 years, or delete it sooner if you ask.
  • Our backups are fully cleared of your data within 30 days.

Questions? Mail hello@tatties.app. More in our privacy policy.
