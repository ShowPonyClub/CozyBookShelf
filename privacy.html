Privacy policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Tatties is local-first. Your data stays on your device by default - nothing is sent to a server unless you explicitly create an account and choose to sync.

What Tatties stores

Tatties stores the following data locally on your device using IndexedDB - your browser's built-in database:

  • Tattoo info you enter: name, artist, studio, style, date, notes, cost, sessions
  • Photos you upload or take
  • Body and skin tone settings

Nothing is sent to any server by default.

Optional cloud sync

If you create an account, your collection (including photos) is encrypted in transit and stored on a secure server in the EU (Supabase, Ireland). You control when sync happens. You can delete your account and all associated cloud data at any time via Settings → Account.

What we do NOT collect

  • No behavioural tracking: no click paths, no screen tracking, no advertising profiles
  • Usage statistics only if you switch them on yourself (see below); off by default
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No data sharing with third parties

Sharing anonymous usage (optional)

In Settings you can turn on ‘Share anonymous usage’. It is off by default. With it on, the app sends at most one message per day to our own server in the EU containing: a random number the app generates itself (not an account or device id), the date, the app version, your language, whether you opened the 3D view, and which band your number of tattoos falls into (for example “4-10”). No names, no photos, no tattoo data, no location, no click tracking. We use it for one question: do people come back. Switch it off and sending stops immediately; rows sent earlier contain nothing that identifies you.

Waitlist & notify-me

If you signed up for early access on tatties.app, we store your email address to notify you when the app launches. We will not use it for anything else, and you can request deletion at any time by emailing hello@tatties.app.

Your rights (GDPR)

  • Access & correct - all your data is visible and editable in the app
  • Delete local data - use “Erase all local data” in Settings, or clear your browser's site data for tatties.app
  • Delete cloud data - delete your account in Settings → Account
  • Portability - use the Export function to download a copy of all your data
  • Waitlist removal - email us and we remove you within 48 hours

Data controller

Tatties - hello@tatties.app
