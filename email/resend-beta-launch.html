Tatties is ready for you

A while back you left your email on tatties.app to hear when there was something to try. Today's the day: the Tatties beta is live.

Tatties lets you see where your next tattoo fits before you commit - add a design to a 3D body, place it on an arm or thigh, and keep a private log of the ink you already have.

Open the beta

Private by design. Everything you add stays on your device. No account needed - syncing is optional.

Works offline. Add it to your home screen and it runs like a real app - on iPhone via Share then Add to Home Screen, on Android via Chrome's menu then Install app.

It's a beta. Things may look off or break. That's exactly why you're getting this early.

What would help us most: add a tattoo from a real photo, or from an image or flash design (the crop tools are the heart of the app), spin the 3D body, and try it on the body part you'd actually get inked.
